## Platform Overview

Paperless Parts provides cloud-native quoting software for job shops and contract manufacturers. The platform streamlines the entire quote-to-cash process with automated costing, pricing logic, and secure collaboration tools.

### Core Features

- **Geometric Pricing Engine**: Patented technology that analyzes part geometry to automate costing and pricing
- **P3L Pricing Language**: Configurable pricing logic that adapts to any shop workflow
- **Manufacturability Analysis**: Automated DFM warnings for CNC machining, sheet metal, and additive processes
- **Requirements Review**: AI-powered Wingman™ functionality for capturing workflow from prints
- **3D Part Viewer**: Browser-based CAD viewing without native software
- **PDF Viewer**: Search within PDFs for tolerances, MIL-SPECs, and ASTM specifications
- **BOM Builder**: Automatic extraction of bill of materials from CAD assemblies
- **Quote Analytics**: Win rate analysis, turnaround tracking, and profitability insights
- **Secure File Sharing**: ITAR-compliant file sharing with access controls and expiration
- **Team Collaboration**: In-context chat, task assignment, and workflow management
- **Nesting**: Built-in sheet metal nesting for material optimization

## Security & Compliance

Paperless Parts has designed its compliance program to support customers requiring CMMC Level 2 who use Paperless Parts as an External Cloud Service Provider (CSP) to handle Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI).

### Defense Industrial Base Approach

1. **Infrastructure**: Built on AWS GovCloud (FedRAMP High authorized), located entirely in the United States
2. **System Security Plan**: Based on FedRAMP Moderate baseline, third-party assessment completed September 2023
3. **Personnel**: 100% US Persons system administrators and support staff located in the United States

### Certifications

- ITAR Registered
- FedRAMP Moderate Equivalent (verified by authorized FedRAMP assessor)
- CMMC Level 2 Support
- DFARS 7012 Compliant
- SOC 2
